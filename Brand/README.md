# just-right identity

## Name

The product name is always `just-right`: lowercase, with the hyphen, including at the start of a sentence. Internal `com.opendente.*` identifiers remain unchanged so existing privileged-helper installations continue to work.

## Mark

Two balanced forms hold one point at the exact midpoint. The dark forms represent the operating range; the emerald point is the chosen threshold—the amount that is just right.

The mark is intentionally not a literal battery. The menu-bar icon remains a functional battery indicator; the brand mark belongs to the app icon, documentation, and product identity.

## Colour

- Emerald `#1A8561`: brand accent and selected threshold
- Charcoal `#181818`: primary mark
- Warm white `#F7F3EA`: icon field

Emerald is the only brand accent. Orange and red remain reserved for warning and critical states.

## Typography

Use the macOS system typeface. Product copy is sentence case, direct, and compact. Do not convert the product name to title case.
